## What the exam actually tests

The product surface a heavy business user touches every day:

- **Projects**: persistent workspaces combining custom instructions with uploaded knowledge sources
- **Artifacts**: the dedicated output panel for documents and structured deliverables
- **Research mode** and **connectors** to tools like Google Drive and Gmail
- **Model tiers**: choosing between Claude models based on the cost, speed, and quality a task demands

Just as much weight sits on judgment, and the domain table shows it: the single largest domain is evaluating outputs. That means checking for accuracy, spotting hallucinations (confident but fabricated claims), knowing when a task requires human review, and recognizing which use cases an organization's data-sensitivity and AI policies rule out.

CCAO-F fits professionals in operations, marketing, project management, education, and communications who use Claude regularly and want a credential that proves it. If you sit between business and engineering and are unsure whether this or the developer exam fits, the deciding question is whether your work happens inside the claude.ai app or in code that calls the API.

## Study path (~3 weeks part-time)

1. **Week 1: official baseline.** Join the [Claude Partner Network](https://claude.com/partners) (free), complete the Partner Academy associate courses, and read the exam guide.
2. **Week 2: deliberate product practice.** Work through the claude.ai Help Center systematically, and use every feature the blueprint names on your own real work: build a Project with knowledge sources, produce Artifacts, run research mode, connect a data source, and compare model tiers on the same task.
3. **Week 3: calibrate.** Full-length timed practice tests, per-domain review, and a final pass over governance and evaluation scenarios, which trip up candidates who prepared only on features.

## Common mistakes

- Treating it as a features quiz. The two judgment domains (output evaluation, governance and responsible use) are 36% of the exam.
- Preparing on the developer platform. The exam covers claude.ai the product; API knowledge earns nothing here.
- Letting the credential lapse: renewal is free before the 12-month mark and a full-price proctored retake after it.
