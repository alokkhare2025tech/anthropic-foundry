# 7. Final Exam Strategy    

### For each question, ask:
---
1. What failed? 
- Tool choice, loop control, context, schema, workflow order, CI, review precision, escalation?
---
2. Does this require a guarantee? 
- If yes, choose deterministic enforcement.
---
3. Is the failure upstream or downstream? 
- Missing topic areas often mean coordinator decomposition, not synthesis failure.
---
4. Is the output structurally wrong or semantically wrong? 
- Schema vs validation.
---
5. Is this a scope/configuration question? 
- User/project/directory/path-specific.
---
6. Is the answer over-engineered? 
- Prefer the direct Claude-native mechanism.
---
7. Is the option solving a different problem?
- Reject it even if technically valid.
