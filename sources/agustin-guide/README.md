# Claude Certified Architect — Foundations (CCA-F): Community Study Guide

An open, community-oriented study guide for Anthropic's **Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCA-F)** exam. These are condensed, exam-ready notes synthesized from Anthropic's public engineering blog, the official exam guide, the Claude Agent SDK cookbook, and publicly available community resources.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This guide contains **no** real exam questions, no answer keys, and no proprietary or memorized exam content. It is a conceptual study aid covering the *patterns and mental models* the exam tests. Always follow Anthropic's certification terms and confidentiality agreement. Verify exam format details against the current official exam guide — they can change.

## Contents

1. **[study-plan.md](./study-plan.md)** — A structured ~20-day prep plan: domain weightings, the 6 exam scenarios, hands-on Agent SDK exercises, and a day-by-day schedule template you can adapt to your own timeline.
2. **[cheatsheet.md](./cheatsheet.md)** — The core exam-day reference: the mental models that win the exam, the recurring anti-patterns, and quick references for the Agent SDK, MCP, Claude Code, prompt engineering, the Batch API, and context management.
3. **[anthropic-refs.md](./anthropic-refs.md)** — Condensed notes from the two Anthropic engineering pages most cited as essential reading for the Multi-Agent Research scenario, quoted for recall.

## Who this is for

Anyone preparing for the CCA-F who wants a high-signal, conceptual companion to the official materials. It pairs best with hands-on Agent SDK practice — reading alone won't get you there.
